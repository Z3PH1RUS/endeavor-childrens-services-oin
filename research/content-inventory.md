## 4. I Am Who? Mission (`/i-am-who/i-am-who-mission/`)

### Headlines
- "I Am Who? invites children into creative activities designed to enhance their well being."
- Cited by UNICEF as a "Best Practice" therapeutic tool

### Body Themes
- Child's life book kept forever
- Author Janice Neilson — 30+ years with vulnerable children in 10 countries
- Arts activities: music, movement, creative drama, drawing/painting/collage/photography, story listening/telling, personal reflection, poetry writing
- Illustrations by Diana Magnuson
- Psycho-social intervention need alongside material needs (nutrition, housing, health care, school, family support)
- **Evaluation data (2007–2009):** 400+ children over one year
  - Depression: 89% → 5%
  - Self-esteem: 9% → 90%

- "Whenever I open my I Am Who? book, I feel my father is close to me."

### Body Themes
- Year of uplifting integrated activities: literature, art, music, movement, drawing, painting, collage, photography, poetry, creative writing, drama, personal reflection
- For school-age children in group or one-on-one settings

- Target populations: grief/loss, divorced/blended families, adopted, foster care, immigrant, homeless, children with disabilities, counseling
