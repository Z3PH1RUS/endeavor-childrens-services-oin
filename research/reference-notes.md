# Sheltering Arms — Design Reference Notes

**Reference site:** https://shelteringarmsforkids.com/  
**Purpose:** Layout, typography, and interaction inspiration only. No copy, logos, or photos reused.

*Observed September 4, 2026. These are pattern notes for the Endeavor redesign, not a reproduction.*

---

## Overall Feel

- **Modern nonprofit polish** — Feels current, trustworthy, and action-oriented
- **Warm but professional** — Orange/warm accent palette conveys energy without feeling corporate
- **Content-forward** — Large typography and clear hierarchy let mission statements breathe
- **Generous whitespace** — Sections have room; nothing feels cramped or dated

## Layout Patterns

### Hero Section
- Full-width hero with bold headline and supporting subhead
- Dual messaging: emotional tagline above, mission statement as H1
- Strong primary CTA visible without scrolling ("Apply to Enroll", "Make A Donation")
- Background imagery with overlay for text contrast

### Navigation
- Clean horizontal nav with clear labels
- Sticky or prominent header with logo left, nav right
- Dropdown menus for program sections
- Mobile hamburger with full-screen or slide-out menu
- Donate CTA often highlighted in nav or as standalone button

### Card-Based Content
- "What's New" section uses card grid for news/updates
- Each card: image or icon, headline, short excerpt, link/CTA
- Programs and impact areas presented as visual cards with icons or photos
- Cards have subtle shadow, rounded corners, hover lift

### Section Rhythm
- Alternating background colors (white / light gray / brand color blocks)
- Clear section headings with supporting paragraph
- Stats or impact numbers highlighted in dedicated bands
- Sponsor/partner logos in organized grid at page bottom

### Footer
- Multi-column: quick links, contact info, social icons
- Newsletter signup form ("Stay Informed")
- Copyright and legal links
- Consistent brand color background

## Typography

- **Serif display font** for headlines (ITC Century / CenturyStd-Bold observed) — adds warmth and authority
- **Sans-serif body** for readability
- Large H1/H2 sizes with tight line-height on headlines
- Comfortable body line-height (1.6–1.8)
- Clear weight contrast between headings and body

## Color & Contrast

- Primary accent: warm orange (`#F05522`, `#ff6900` range)
- Dark text on light backgrounds for accessibility
- White text on colored hero/footer sections
- Accent color used sparingly for buttons and links, not overwhelming

## Buttons & CTAs

- Rounded, filled primary buttons in accent color
- High contrast white text on colored buttons
- Secondary/outline buttons for less prominent actions
- CTAs repeated at natural decision points (hero, mid-page, footer)
- Action verbs: "Apply", "Donate", "Give", "Learn More", "Tickets"

## Imagery

- Authentic photography of children, families, and staff
- Images cropped consistently (aspect ratios maintained in cards)
- Overlays on hero images for text legibility
- No stock-photo cliché feeling — community-specific

## Mobile-First Observations

- Responsive grid collapses cards to single column
- Touch-friendly nav toggle
- Hero text scales down gracefully
- CTAs remain thumb-reachable

## Patterns Applied to Endeavor Redesign

| Sheltering Arms Pattern | Endeavor Adaptation |
|------------------------|---------------------|
| Bold hero + mission H1 | Hero with tagline + "One child at a time" framing |
| Card grid for programs | I Am Who? sub-programs as cards + services cards |
| Prominent Donate CTA | Sticky nav Donate button → PayPal link |
| Clean section spacing | 80–120px section padding, alternating backgrounds |
| Serif + sans pairing | Source Serif 4 + DM Sans (similar warmth, distinct typefaces) |
| Impact stats band | I Am Who evaluation data (89%→5% depression) |
| Footer with contact + social | Phone, Facebook, quick links |
| News/updates cards | "Our Impact" and program highlights (no fake news) |

## What We Did NOT Copy

- Sheltering Arms name, logo, or brand colors
- Any Sheltering Arms copy, statistics, or program names
- Photography or visual assets
- Atlanta-specific content or center listings
