Our Programs

Your support makes a lasting difference in the lives of orphans and vulnerable children. Endeavor develops innovative children's services programs while providing food, health care access, and education.

I Am Who? Program

Access to creative arts, therapeutic activities, and a life book to treasure are fundamentals every child should have — yet many vulnerable children lack the psycho-social support they need alongside material aid.

Donate NowLearn More

Food & Nutrition

Endeavor provides food for hungry children in the communities we serve, addressing immediate nutritional needs as part of a holistic approach to child well-being.

Donate NowLearn More

Health Care Access

Connecting vulnerable children to health care is essential. Endeavor works to ensure children receive the medical support they need to thrive.

Education Support

School access opens doors to the future. Endeavor supports children's education alongside programs that build confidence, hope, and self-esteem.

Who We Serve

With a special focus on children living in extreme poverty, we draw upon the expertise of professionals in health, nutrition, education, law, psychology, and art who share a dedication to improving the lives of children most in need — anywhere in the world.

Global Reach

Guided by a community of professionals, Endeavor serves orphans and vulnerable children across continents.

Holistic Care

Beyond material needs, children deeply need sustained psycho-social support alongside food, health care, and education.

Volunteer-Led

Our board, staff, and expert advisors all volunteer their time and expertise.
